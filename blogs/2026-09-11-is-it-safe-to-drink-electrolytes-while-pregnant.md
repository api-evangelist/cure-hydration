Pregnancy changes almost everything about how your body uses water. Blood volume increases, fluid demands go up, and if morning sickness is part of the picture, you may be losing fluids faster than you can comfortably replace them. That's why many expecting parents wonder whether electrolyte drinks are safe during pregnancy.
