You woke up with a headache, dry mouth, and that fog that says last night caught up with you. Your first instinct is to chug water, but plain water alone does not replace what alcohol pulled out of your system: sodium, potassium, and the fluid balance your body needs. Electrolyte drinks can be useful for replacing fluids and minerals, but the ingredients and amounts vary widely between products.
