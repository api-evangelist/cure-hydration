Kids are especially vulnerable to dehydration due to their fast metabolism and small size, which leads them to lose and replace water and electrolytes at a faster rate. For this reason, it’s important to consider supplementing with electrolytes. To help, this guide will provide an overview of why kids get dehydrated and how often they should supplement with electrolytes.
