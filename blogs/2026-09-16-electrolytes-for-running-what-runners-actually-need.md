There's a question most runners eventually ask, usually mid-run and usually too late: "Should I have taken something before this?" Whether you're training for your first 5K or your fourth half marathon, hydration is one of those things that's easy to overlook until it catches up with you. And plain water, as helpful as it is, doesn't replace what you actually lose through sweat. Here's what runners need to know about electrolytes, when to take them, and how to keep it simple.
